Road to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. The list is long, not hard.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

    Finish the five chapters and do side content as it appears. Story progress already counts toward the 100% checklist, and there are no mission medals to worry about. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, Progress > Stats > 100% Completion opens. It is closer to 60% true completion: most lines need only 70–80%, and any 70 of the 80 challenges will do. Only robberies, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s hunts need everything.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers and cleanup about 10 hours

    Strangers & Freaks are not part of the 100% checklist, so finish the eight counting strands and mop up anything left. By now most trophies will already have popped.

Before you start

  • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, the first morning it is offered.
  • Cheats switch trophies off (and saving), so keep them off on your trophy save.
  • Three trophies have no verified method yet at launch (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, Ten Pin). They are marked unconfirmed below — do not chase rumors for them.
