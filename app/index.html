The road to the platinum

Around 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Long, not hard.

  1. 1

    Play the story 60–80 hours

    Finish the five chapters and take side content as it appears. Story progress already counts toward the 100% checklist, and there are no mission medals to chase. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.

  2. 2

    Work the 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, Progress > Stats > 100% Completion opens. It is closer to 60% true completion: most lines ask only 70–80%, and any 70 of the 80 challenges will do. Only robberies, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s hunts ask for everything.

  3. 3

    Strangers and cleanup about 10 hours

    Strangers & Freaks sit outside the 100% checklist, so finish the eight strands that count and mop up what is left. Most trophies will already have popped by now.

Watch out for

  • One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, the first morning it is offered.
  • Cheats turn trophies off (and saving), so keep them off on your trophy save.
  • Three trophies have no verified method yet at launch (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, Ten Pin). They are marked unconfirmed below — do not chase rumors for them.
